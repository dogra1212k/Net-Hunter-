ABOUT & SAFETY
==============

Net Hunter Guide is an offline learning companion for Termux, Kali Linux and NetHunter basics.

WHAT THIS APP IS FOR
- Learning Linux commands
- Understanding networking
- Practicing on localhost
- Reading tool notes
- Running safe labs
- Building troubleshooting habits
- Tracking lesson progress
- Continuing the last opened lesson

SAFE USE
Use commands and security tools only on:
- your own device
- localhost
- your own lab
- systems where you have explicit permission

GOOD PRACTICE
- Read --help and man pages first.
- Understand what a command does before running it.
- Keep backups of important files.
- Record your lab steps and results.
- Learn remediation as well as detection.
- Do not use random commands copied from the internet blindly. Humanity has already suffered enough from that habit.

APP BEHAVIOR
Lessons are bundled inside the app and open in the app itself.
The home screen search filters lesson buttons by title.
Progress and the last opened lesson are stored locally on the device.
Progress can be reset only after a confirmation prompt.
No browser is required for offline lesson reading.
